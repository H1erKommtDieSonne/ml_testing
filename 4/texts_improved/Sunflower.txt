The common sunflower ( Helianthus annuus ) is a large annual forb in the daisy family Asteraceae . The most common varieties have large yellow orange flowers heads, that are a tall stem. The flowers produce seeds in a floret, that follows the fibonacci sequence . The domesticated form of common sunflower is harvested for its edible seeds , which come in two types: oil and confectionery seeds. Oilseed sunflowers are widely grown globally and represent the fourth most used vegetable oil in the world. They also are used widely as bird food or as food for livestock . In contrast, confectionery sunflower seeds are often eaten as a snack food or in baking. Though first domesticate in the Americas, production of sunflowers commercially is concentrated in Russia, Ukraine, and the European Union. There also are horticultural sunflower varieties that are used as plantings in domestic gardens for aesthetics. In some cultivation practices sunflowers are used for phytoremediation , removing chemicals from the soil, and as cover crop because root exudates help with suppression of certain kinds of weeds.
